Reading · Optical sizing

A letter isn’t a drawing of a letter. It’s a weight on the page.

At 12 points, a bold stroke that looked even at 72 will feel skinny. Optical sizing is the quiet cheat: you thicken, open, and slightly widen the same design so it still reads as itself when it shrinks.

You don’t need the history of metal type for this block. You need to see two sizes of the same face and notice which one looks hungry.
