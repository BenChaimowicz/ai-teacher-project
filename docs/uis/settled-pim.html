Reading · Left-hand independence

Your left hand is not a quieter right hand.

It has its own job. On the piano that usually means a pattern that repeats while the right hand tells the story. If you try to “also play the melody down there,” both hands get busy and neither one settles.

Today is just a five-note shape. Slow enough that you can feel the thumb land without looking.
