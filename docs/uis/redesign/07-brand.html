Meet Nibbles

The Knibbler mark is Nibbles' silhouette in a graduation cap: the head shape, ears and round cheeks, with the cap cut out of it by a thin gap. No face, so it stays crisp down to a 16px tab icon. The full face belongs to the companion in the app (and to the loader). The mark and wordmark take the current accent pair.

sidebar companion
  • A real speech bubble. Filled, borderless, with a tail up to Nibbles, so it can't be mistaken for a text field.
  • Eyes follow the pointer, eased and clamped. When the mouse is still, Nibbles glances around now and then.
  • Hover twitches an ear. Click gives a small squish and the next line from the page's context list (nibblesLines([...])).
  • The ring is today's goal (18 of 20 min). No points or currency: just the streak and today's minutes.
  • All motion turns off with the system's reduced-motion setting.
