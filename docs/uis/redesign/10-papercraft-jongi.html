Folio

The whole platform as a pop-up book. Every screen is a page you can turn.

Surfaces are cut sheets of ink-navy and plum paper stacked on a dark desk. You get depth from real shadows between the layers, not from glass or glow.

One warm apricot does the talking. A bookmark-red ribbon marks your place, your streak and anything that needs you. Gold thread is kept for wins.

The companion is a hamster folded from the same paper, with a tilted scholar cap and a bookmark for a tail.

Lesson 7 · Cosmology

Yggdrasil and the nine worlds

An ash tree so large that the whole of creation hangs in its branches.

Norse myth doesn't picture the universe as a sphere or a flat disc. It pictures a tree. Yggdrasil is an evergreen ash whose branches spread over the sky, and every world, from the gods' hall to the frozen dark, sits somewhere along its trunk or among its roots.

Three roots, three wells

In Snorri Sturluson's account, the tree stands on three roots. One reaches the gods in Asgard, where the Well of Urd waters it each day. One reaches the giants and Mímir's well of wisdom, the well Odin gave an eye to drink from. The third sinks into misty Niflheim, where the dragon Níðhöggr gnaws at it from below.Gylfaginning 15

◆ Worth knowing

No medieval source lists all nine worlds in one place. The tidy list you see in books and games is a modern reconstruction stitched together from several poems.

The tree is always under strain. Deer strip its leaves, serpents bite its roots, and a squirrel called Ratatoskr runs up and down the trunk carrying insults between the eagle at the top and the dragon at the bottom.

04

The direction, briefly

  • Why paper. Studying already happens on paper: pages, bookmarks, margin notes. Folio borrows that material instead of the glassy "AI" look, so it feels calm and trustworthy, and the dark theme reads as a desk at night.
  • One rule for depth. Every surface is a sheet, and the higher it sits, the longer its shadow. Tiles that need you are stacked, and selected ones lift and catch an apricot rim. There's no blur and no gradients pretending to be light.
  • Ribbons carry meaning. Progress, status, streaks and "your pick" are all notched bookmark ribbons. Jongi's tail is the same ribbon, so "it saved your place" is literally true.
  • Motion is pop-up book. Screens unfold from the fold line, tiles lift with a small overshoot, and the companion's layers lag each other by 40–80ms. Squash and stretch only applies to the whole body, and never by more than about 10%.
  • Built to animate in Rive. Each paper layer is a Z-ordered group on one artboard. A state machine runs Idle (breathing loop, random blinks, an ear-flick layer), Thinking, a one-shot Win trigger that returns to Idle, and Resting after inactivity. lookX and lookY inputs drive the parallax and the pupils. The loader is its own 64px artboard.
  • Dopamine, folded small. Seeds count up, the streak ribbon and the minutes ring (cut into paper segments) fill up, and paper confetti flutters with real two-sided flips. Rewards feel tactile and stay quiet.
