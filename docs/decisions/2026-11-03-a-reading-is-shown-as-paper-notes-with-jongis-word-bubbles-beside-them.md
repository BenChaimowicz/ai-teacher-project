# A Reading is shown as paper notes, a hard word opens Jongi's bubble on hover, and Citations are small superscripts

**Why:** With the SEN-48 glossary in place, the reading screen was still one big block of text with a word list in a side panel, and the Citation numbers were chunky key-tab chips that broke the line and pulled the eye. The product owner wants the Papercraft feel on the reading itself: paragraphs split into notes, smaller and less bold text, and the hard words as tiny bubbles from Jongi.

Each Reading section is now a paper note (a sheet with the `--sheet` shadow and the mock's note corner), stacked down the page. A hard word stays highlighted in the text, and hovering, focusing or tapping it opens its definition as a small speech bubble from Jongi, the same on every screen size and in the Book layout; there is no side list and no fold under the Lesson any more. Citations are small superscript numbers in the accent colour, with the hover tooltip kept. The data a Reading stores does not change; this is how Study draws it.

We turned down bubbles placed in the margin beside the note where each word first appears, which was the first cut of this: a column of bubbles next to the text interrupts the flow of reading, and the owner wants nothing on the page until the Learner asks for it. We also turned down keeping the side list and only shrinking the Citations, because a list far from the sentence is what the Learner has to leave the line for. The cost is that the Learner sees no list of the Lesson's hard words at a glance, and a touch Learner has to tap a word to learn there is a definition.
