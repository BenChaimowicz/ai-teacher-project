# The regular research tier is Parallel `core` plus You.com Research lite; deluxe stays Parallel Pro plus You.com Research

**Why:** A regular tier needs two cheaper adapters that keep spec §5.3's two independent engines. Parallel `core` ($0.025) plus You.com Research lite ($0.012) costs $0.037 a Reading, against $0.15 on deluxe. Both return long page excerpts, and a Reading is written from those excerpts alone.

We turned down You.com Web Search plus Research lite ($0.017). It sends both calls to one vendor, which breaks §5.3. We also turned down Web Search plus Parallel `core` ($0.03). Web Search matched the dearest tier on the Starting Level fact-check, but it returns short snippets and has never fed a Reading.

**What it costs:** a Course rated mainstream gets thinner packs. In SEN-120, `core` returned about a third of Pro's excerpt text. The test-Course run on the regular tier (`2026-11-02-the-regular-research-tier-is-proven-on-the-test-courses-before-it-goes-live`) is the check on that.
