# A Reading holds at most two required images, from its Blueprint slot, and at most two optional ones, from its writer

The Blueprint author may require up to two images on a Reading slot, which leaves room for both grip and stroke in one drum Lesson. The Reading writer may add up to two optional images of its own. Required images are always placed. Optional ones are dropped on any miss or failed check, as in SEN-55.

**What we turned down:** keeping SEN-55's single optional image next to the required ones, so a Lesson with several things worth showing could show only one of them. A single shared cap of three, where the writer fills whatever room the required images leave, which makes how many images a writer may add depend on the Blueprint.

**What it costs:** up to four images per Reading, so up to four vision checks plus repairs before a Lesson is checkpointed. A Lesson that needs a third required image has to be split into two slots.
