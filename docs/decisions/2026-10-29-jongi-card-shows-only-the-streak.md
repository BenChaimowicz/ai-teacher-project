# Jongi's sidebar card keeps one chip, the streak, and gets more room

**Why:** The card felt cramped, with two stat chips squeezed under Jongi's name. We compared five layouts at sidebar width: roomier padding, a stats strip along the bottom, a quiet text line, a goal bar, and the current card. We chose the roomier layout and dropped the minutes counter. Today's minutes still show in the goal ring around Jongi and in its hover title, so nothing is lost. The speech area now reserves room for one line instead of two. A two-line remark makes the card a little taller, which is better than every one-line remark leaving a gap at the bottom.
