# While reading, Jongi's right paw props his cheek instead of resting on the left page, so the turning page never crosses it

In `Jongi.IdleReading` the right arm bends at the elbow. The elbow rests on the floor past the book's top-left corner, and the paw cups his cheek and moves with his head. Only the left paw stays on the book, and it still flicks the page over. This changes one detail of `2026-10-29-idle-gets-a-second-variant-reading-on-his-stomach`, where both forearms rested on the pages.

**Why:** The turning page lands on the left page, which is exactly where the right paw rested. Cut paper has to draw the page either in front of the arm or behind it. Drawn behind, it looked as if the page cut straight through his arm. Ben compared five mocked options and picked this one:

- **Cheek prop (picked):** fixes the clash through the pose itself.
- **Standing book:** the book stands upright and hides his paws. It needs a whole new book, and a cheated page turn arching over the top edge.
- **Paw hops out of the way:** the paw lifts on every turn. Cheapest, but it only works while the timing holds, and it adds motion to a loop that's meant to be calm.
- **Narrower book between his paws:** shaped like a real book. It needs a second book model, because the wide one is his seat in the other moods.
- **Chin in both paws:** needs two bent arms and a long reach for the flick.

**What it costs:** While reading, the right arm is its own bent arm: an upper arm on the lying body, a forearm that bends between the floor and the cheek, and a paw. It is not the straight arm the other moods use, so refinements to that arm don't carry over to the reading pose. For about 0.1 seconds mid-turn, the lifted page passes in front of the propped paw. The silhouette is now lopsided, with one elbow out to the side, where before it was symmetric.
