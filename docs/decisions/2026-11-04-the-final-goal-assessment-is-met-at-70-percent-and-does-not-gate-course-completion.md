# The final goal assessment counts as met at 70%, and the Course completes after one scored attempt whatever the result

**Why:** Bands on the best score: met at `correct / n ≥ 0.70`, partly met from 0.50 up to 0.70, not met below 0.50. The 70% bar is the same as the Quiz pass bar, so the product has one number for "you got it". Under 50% is well above the 25% you get by guessing, so it means the goal really was not reached. We turned down a stricter 80% for "met", which would be harsher than the Quizzes that already gated every Lesson. The final assessment stays out of Progress (completed Lessons / total Lessons). The Course counts as complete once every Lesson is complete and the Learner has made one scored attempt. We turned down requiring a pass to complete, because a "not met" Learner would sit at an unfinished Course forever. The "not met" flow is the answer to failing, not a locked door. Retakes are unlimited and the best score is kept, like Quizzes. The cost is that a completed Course does not prove the goal was met; the met / partly met / not met result carries that instead.
