# "Take quiz" lives in the Study sidebar and on a card after the last Reading a Quiz covers, with one Bao nudge there

**Why:** Once Next stops landing on a Quiz, the Learner needs a place to find it. The sidebar row is where they look for it later; the card at the end of the last Reading the Quiz covers catches them at the natural moment without blocking Next. We turned down a floating button, which would clutter the Reading. On that card Bao says one gentle line suggesting the Quiz, only while that Quiz has not been taken, and never repeats it. We turned down staying silent, because without any nudge most Learners would never open a Quiz. The cost is one more line of companion copy at the end of a Chapter.
