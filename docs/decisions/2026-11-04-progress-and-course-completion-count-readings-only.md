# Progress and Course completion count Readings only; Quiz scores are shown on their own

**Why:** Quizzes are now optional (SEN-133). Today Progress counts a Quiz only once it is passed, and a Course is complete only when every Quiz is passed, so a Learner who skips Quizzes could never reach 100% or finish the Course. We turned down keeping Quizzes in the count, which would quietly punish the skip we just said costs nothing. The cost: "Course complete" no longer proves the Learner checked their understanding anywhere. Quiz results stay visible as the "quizzes passed" stat in the Study sidebar and the per-Quiz best scores on the Course complete screen.
