# The regular research tier runs both test Courses end to end before the rule goes live

**Why:** On 2026-11-01 we kept Parallel Pro because the cheaper `core` pack carried about a third of the excerpt text, and a Reading is written from its pack alone (see `2026-11-01-primary-research-stays-on-parallel-pro`). A regular tier brings that risk back for every Course the rubric rates mainstream. Before the rule is turned on, Norse and Gram run end to end on the regular tier, and their repair counts and the Judge's results are compared against the 2026-11-01 re-run.

We turned down shipping and then watching repairs per tier in the Usage Console. That is faster, but early Learners carry the risk.

**What it costs:** about an hour of run time and a few dollars. With two Courses, the comparison will show a gross failure but not a subtle quality drop.
