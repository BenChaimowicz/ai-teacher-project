# The sidebar is built around one current course, the one the Learner opened most recently

**Why:** Ben's review said courses read like ordinary rows in the sidebar list. We expect a Learner to have only a course or two at a time, so rather than giving all five rows equal weight, the sidebar gives the current course a large card (progress, the next lesson, Continue), and the rest shrink to quiet one-line links, with Home still managing the full list. We turned down making every row a bigger two-line card, Ben's first suggestion, because it still presents courses as a list to manage. The current course is the one opened most recently, not the newest or the furthest along, because that is the one the Learner is most likely coming back to. The cost: a Learner who really does juggle several courses sees the others smaller and one click further away.
