# The rebound-stroke loop of spec §7.2 is replaced by a product-owned SVG strip of stills, drawn in SEN-115 and seeded in its own Media category

The drum Course needs stroke media a Lesson cannot ship without, and the seeded catalog has grip stills but nothing for the stroke. SEN-115 draws one schematic strip of 3 to 4 frames: the stick held up, the strike, the rebound, and the stick back at its starting height. It shows a stick and a drumhead with no hands. It is seeded with licence `product_owned` in a new `drum-stroke` Media category, like the notation diagrams, so the drum Course has stroke media as soon as this ticket lands.

**What we turned down:** filming a short silent clip ourselves, which needs animation support (the `animation` kind and a still frame for the vision check) that no ticket is building. Generating a loop, which breaks this ticket's no-generation rule and waits on a generation pipeline. Deciding on stills but leaving the drawing to SEN-47, which would leave the drum Course waiting on a seed ticket.

**What it costs:** a strip of stills shows the motion less clearly than a loop does. It is also original artwork that a drummer has to check before the drum Course is signed off, like the notation diagrams.
