# A goal is scholarly only when general references cannot carry it

**Why:** In the regular-tier test run, Luna rated the focused Norse goal scholarly because "careful accounts rely on specialist interpretation of the sources". Under `auto` that would have sent Norse, which is mainstream and our own example of a well-covered subject, to deluxe research. The question now asks whether encyclopedias, textbooks and reputable explainers are *unable* to carry the goal, and states that specialists also writing on a topic does not make it scholarly. Gram was rated not scholarly before the change and should stay so.

We turned down dropping the scholarly question and keeping only niche. A goal can be covered by many general pages and still be taught well only from specialist ones.

**What it costs:** a goal near the line, such as an advanced clinical or legal topic with good general coverage, now gets regular research. The Judge and repair loop catch a thin draft, at about $0.05 a repair.
