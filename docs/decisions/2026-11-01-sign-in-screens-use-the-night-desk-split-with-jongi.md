# The signed-out screens use the "Night desk" split: a navy panel with a large Jongi on the left, and a clean form on the right

The left panel holds the logo, the headline "Learn anything, starting from what you already know.", a short line on what Knibbler does, and three small points: built around your goal, starts at your level, every fact has a source. A large full-body Jongi stands on his book above a paper desk. His eyes follow the pointer, he thinks while a request is running, and he cheers when it works. The right side is the form, without a card around it: a Password / Email me a code switch, fields, and the apricot button. Sign up, check your email, forgot password and reset password reuse the same frame with their own copy. On a phone the panel shrinks to a band above the form.

**What we turned down:** fourteen other directions from the sign-in options canvas. Among them were a tilted apricot paper sheet (B), Jongi greeting you in chat bubbles (C), signing in as a conversation (J), a front door (M), a postcard (N) and a balloon ride (O). We also ruled out anything school-themed.

**What it costs:** the most conventional of the options, so its personality rests on Jongi moving. The first build's centered card is replaced.
