# Washi tape holds down a few chosen sheets, each at its own angle

**Why:** Yarden picked washi tape from five papercraft touches (dog-ear, tape, torn edge, an askew sheet underneath, stitching) to push the papercraft theme further. It goes on a few sheets only: Jongi's sidebar card, home's Continue card, the streak box and the needs-you cards. Tape on everything would stop reading as a craft detail. Each strip always sits in the middle of the top edge and has no pattern. We tried random spots, corner wraps, stripes and dots, and they looked too busy. Identical tape looks stamped, though, so a strip's tint (cream, apricot, plum or gold), width and angle (2–7°, either way) come from a seed, usually the course key. The seed keeps a strip from jumping between renders, and a new course gets its own angle. Cards that clip their edges (the hero, Jongi's card) carry the tape on a wrapper around them.
