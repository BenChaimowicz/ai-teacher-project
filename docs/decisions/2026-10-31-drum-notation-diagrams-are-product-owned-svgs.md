# The drum notation slots are covered by three or four simple product-owned SVG diagrams in the seed catalog

The drum Course (spec §7.2) needs notation media: a kit notation legend, a generic 4/4 rock beat, and 4/4 against 6/8. No free Commons file covers them, and SEN-115 makes a catalog miss on required media a Lesson failure with no generation fallback. So we draw them ourselves as plain SVGs, seed them with licence `product_owned`, and they sit in the catalog like any other library file. None of them is, or resembles, a chart of "Iris"; they are generic patterns.

**What we turned down:** keeping SEN-47 to curated third-party files only and leaving notation to a later ticket on generated media. That would have left the drum Course unable to ship its notation Readings until generation exists, which the §5.6 order puts last.

**What it costs:** hand-drawn diagrams whose notational accuracy a drummer has to eyeball before the drum Course is signed off, and a small amount of original artwork we now own and maintain.
