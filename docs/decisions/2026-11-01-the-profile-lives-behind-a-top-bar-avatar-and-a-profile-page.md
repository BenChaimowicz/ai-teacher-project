# The Learner's profile opens from an initials avatar at the top right of every screen, and gets its own profile page

The avatar opens a menu with the display name, the email, **Profile & settings** and **Sign out**. The profile page leads with a real header: a big avatar, a name you can edit in place, when you joined, and Courses and Lessons finished. Account settings (password, sign out everywhere) sit below it. The small account row at the foot of the sidebar goes away.

**What we turned down:** keeping the account in the sidebar, either as the first build's foot row, which was too easy to miss, or as a card under Jongi's. The sidebar must never scroll, and the top right is where people look for their account.

**What it costs:** the top bar gains a permanent element on every screen, including Study. The profile header needs counts the API doesn't return yet.
