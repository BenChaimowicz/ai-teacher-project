# A reused Lesson teaches an objective grounding rewrote, softened, as a written one does; only an objective its Sources cannot support at all sends the slot to research

On the reuse path, the slot's objectives are grounded against the reused Lesson's own Sources before the fit pass runs (ADR 0022). An objective that comes back `rewritten` keeps its capability with its unsupported statements softened, and the reused Lesson is fitted and judged against that softened text, exactly as a Lesson written from fresh research would be. An objective that comes back `unsupported` means the reused Lesson cannot teach a capability the slot asks for, so the slot falls through: it is researched then and written like any other Reading.

The live check found this on the first run. All four published Norse Readings predate grounding (published Oct 19 and 25; ADR 0022 landed Oct 28). Re-grounding the World-Tree Reading against its own Sources did not fully support "Interpret the world-tree as a symbol of interconnectedness and cosmic order". Under a rule of "every objective fully supported", most of today's pool would never be reused.

**What we turned down:** falling through on any verdict but `supported`, on the grounds that fresh Sources might support the objective as approved. That is the strictest reading of "adapt never changes the slot's objectives", but an interpretive objective rarely gets a better verdict from new research, and every fall-through pays for research the reuse was meant to save.

**What it costs:** a reused Lesson can teach a softened objective where fresh research might, now and then, have supported the full one. The softening is recorded in the reading step's Pipeline Event, as for any Lesson, and the whole-Course Judge still sees the checkpoint's objectives.
