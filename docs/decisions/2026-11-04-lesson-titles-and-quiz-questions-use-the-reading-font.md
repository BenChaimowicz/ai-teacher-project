# Lesson titles, note headings and quiz questions are set in the reading font, and the reading page's notes and glossary words are quieter

**Why:** Yarden found the reading page too loud and not professional enough, the bold title most of all. The title and quiz questions used Baloo 2 Bold, the round display face; the decision of 2026-10-29 (`2026-10-29-display-font-is-baloo-2`) had already named "a more playful, less editorial tone on lesson titles and quiz questions" as its cost, and the title's "lighter" 600 weight never applied, because only Baloo's 700 is loaded. They are now Manrope at 620, the font the Lesson is read in, so the page uses one typeface. Baloo 2 stays everywhere else, including the wins (score, Course complete, Home's numbers), where playful is the point. On the same page the paper notes keep their shape but sit a shade off the desk with a softer shadow and no tape strip, the body text is a touch dimmer, and a glossary word is a faint apricot tint with an underline instead of a boxed key. We kept the notes rather than setting the text straight on the page, because notes were asked for on 2026-11-03. The Quiz follows, because Yarden asked for it smaller and more luxurious: the question drops to 1.32rem, answers are quiet sheets edged with a hairline that no longer jump on hover or when picked, right and wrong are a thin coloured rim over a faint wash, and the progress pips are thin lines. The streak ribbon and the score card keep their colour and Baloo 2, since they are wins. It costs some of the papercraft character on the reading screen: the tape is gone, and the Lesson title no longer matches the rest of the app's headings.
